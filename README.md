# Focus App Project
Taking control of distractions. By teens, for teens. This product is designed for students who want to improve their relationship with technology. It creates a personalized plan to reduce social media usage, without the confusion and ineffectiveness of setting your own limits. Personalized recommendations and planning guide users to stop unwanted doomscrolling for good. 

# Features
- Customizable micro-intervention when opening blocked apps
- Tracking of screentime averages and monitoring of changes over time
- Based on screentime data imported from phone, creates personalized recommendations for realistic time limits & session frequency
- Rather than having users blindly choose limits, app learns from patterns
- Smart features adapt strategy based on user's adherence to the limits
